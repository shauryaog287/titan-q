# Strategy decision, 27 September 2026 (made BEFORE revealing test-period results)

Evidence: `alpha_research.py`, development period only (formation dates 2015-09 to 2020-12,
NIFTY overlays 2015-09 to 2020-12). Test-period columns were blinded when this was written.

## What development showed

1. No stock-selection signal has a demonstrable edge in the 50-stock universe. Mean rank ICs
   run from -0.033 to +0.030, and the best |t| is 1.33 (1-month reversal). Every top-4 and
   top-8 portfolio's excess over the equal-weight universe is within noise (|t| < 1.5).
2. No exposure-timing rule improved the risk-adjusted return of NIFTY. Buy-and-hold had a
   Sharpe of 0.69; the 200-day trend filter 0.61; volatility targeting 0.60; both 0.56. They
   halved drawdowns but also roughly halved CAGR.
3. Weighting the whole universe: equal weight Sharpe 0.95, inverse volatility 0.96 (with a
   smaller drawdown), HRP 0.78.
4. The v2 baseline was 15% invested on average because the Kelly sizer shrank the ENTIRE
   expected return, the market premium included, toward zero.

## Decision

- Selection mode "all": hold every eligible stock. No alpha tilt (alpha_ic = 0).
- Weights: inverse volatility (HRP stays available as an option).
- Sizing: Kelly with an INFORMATIVE prior on the market's return. Prior mean 10% a year
  (NIFTY 1995-2020 compounded about 11%; 10% is a round, conservative figure from
  pre-test-period history), prior sd 3%, quarter Kelly, capped at 100% of equity.
  Exposure still falls as volatility rises (Kelly divides by variance).
- Shields are kept as catastrophe limits only: CRASH regime -> 0 exposure, a novel regime ->
  x0.5, EVT relative trim x0.5. Routine regime multipliers (BEAR/SIDEWAYS/HIGH_VOL) are set
  to 1.0, because timing overlays lowered returns without improving Sharpe in development.
- The concentrated K-of-N annealer mode stays available (selection_mode "optimize"),
  but it is not the default.

## What this is and is not

This captures the equity market premium through a diversified, volatility-aware, low-cost
portfolio. It is NOT a claim of alpha; development found none. Expected behaviour is
roughly market-like returns with market-like drawdowns, somewhat moderated when volatility
is high.
